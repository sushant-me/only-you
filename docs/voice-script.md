  voice moving through the full stop; only breathe at the feature changes.
- **First person.** “I sign,” “my enrolled voice,” “I built.” You are the owner,
  not a narrator describing a product.
- Say **“R six,” “R one,” “R zero”** — not “R6.”
- On **“zero”** and **“eighteen of eighteen,”** slow down and land it. Do not
  shout; let the number do the work.
