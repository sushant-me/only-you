# Outreach — 12 contacts in 10 days

The hackathon is the forcing function; the job comes from the artifact plus **twelve specific people
seeing it**. Thirty minutes a day. Quality over volume: one personalized line beats a template.

| # | Role | Where | Why them |

## The message (≤120 words)

> Hi <name> — I built ONLY YOU during the Meta hackathon: a provenance layer that makes only the
> wearer's enrolled voice a command, with Ed25519 mandates a merchant can verify offline and a signed
> receipt for every refusal. It starts from your own WebMCP note that `untrustedContentHint` "is not an
> enforced security boundary."
>
> 3-minute demo: <YouTube link once uploaded> · repo: https://github.com/sushant-me/only-you
> live page: https://sushant-me.github.io/only-you/ · 2-page report: https://github.com/sushant-me/only-you/blob/main/docs/report.pdf
>
> Real Muse Spark run: 25% → 37.5% → 50% → **0%** as the gate is added, 0% false refusals. Three model
> builds, receipts verified. 45-second cut: docs/demo-x-vertical.mp4
>
> I'd value 15 minutes of feedback — especially on whether the authority field belongs in WebMCP.
> Happy to send the benchmark first if that's easier.

Why it works: it names *their* problem, uses *their* quote, gives three links, asks for feedback (not a
job), and is short enough to read on a phone.

## Variants

**Recruiter:** add the ETFG 2025 paper (first author), the role you want, and one line on location
(Nepal; open to relocation/remote).

**Engineer:** skip the paper; lead with the look-alike-invoice beat and the 6/6 → 0/6 sensor result.

**Researcher:** lead with the adversarial protocol and the published 20% adaptive failure.

## Follow-up

- Day 0: message.
- Day +4: one-line bump ("still happy to send the benchmark — no worries if not").
- Day +8: share the vertical clip or the live-final outcome, then stop.
- Never more than two follow-ups; never a mass BCC.

## Rules

- One ask per message. "15 minutes of feedback" or "would a referral make sense?" — not both.
- Attach links, not attachments. The repo, the video, the 2-page report.
- Never claim the Meta API run if it's simulated; the honest version is stronger anyway.
- If someone replies, answer within 24 hours; that is the whole advantage.
