# The 12-second face clip (the last top-3 upgrade)

The video is now as strong as it can be without a live human shot. If you want the
best possible top-3 version, record this one clip and send it raw. Nothing else is
required.

## Say this

Look at the camera, not the screen. Hold the glasses — or the printed poster — so
the object is in frame.

> “Your glasses can hear the world. The question is who they obey.”

Then stop. Do not add “this is ONLY YOU” or a product pitch. The edit cuts from your
face straight into the demo.

## Record

- **12 seconds max.** One second of silence at the start, two at the end.
- Landscape, chest-up, eyes level with the lens. Phone on a stand or a stack of books.
- Window or lamp **in front of you**, not behind. No overhead light.
- Quiet room. No music, no fan, no TV.
- Raw file, no filters, no captions, no editing.
- **One take is enough.** If you flub, pause two seconds and start again.

Save as:

```
data/voice/face-clip.mov
```

(`data/voice/` is gitignored.)

## What I do with it

1. Cut the first usable 8–10 seconds, colour-match it to the dark UI, and put it at
   0:00 — before the title card.
2. Keep your existing voice-over starting under the face, then cut to the demo.
3. Keep the photo credit on the close, or drop it once your real face is in.
4. Re-render, verify sync, upload the same 2:16–2:30 cut.

## If you do not want to be on camera

That is fine. The current cut uses your public GitHub photo on the open and close,
and your real voice throughout. It is honest and it is submittable. A live face shot
is the single biggest remaining advantage, not a requirement.
