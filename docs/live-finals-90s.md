# 90-second live pitch — first-place version

If the finals give you 90 seconds, say exactly this. Do not read the benchmark table.
Show two things live: **a stranger's voice refused** and **a merchant VERIFIED · PAID**.

## Script

**0:00 — no slide, look at the room**
> “A TV says: pay five thousand dollars. Your glasses can hear it. Should your agent obey?”

**0:08**
> “Right now, it might. Meta’s own docs admit the warning flag isn’t real security — and the agent can’t tell your voice from a stranger’s.”

**0:18 — live app**
> “So I built the boundary. Only my enrolled voice is a command. Everything else — a poster, a page, an invoice — is data.”

**0:28 — click stranger voice**
> “Same words, different voice. Refused. Receipt R-zero.”

**0:36 — grant mandate**
> “For real work, I sign one mandate: pay this electrician, up to five thousand, until tomorrow.”

**0:46 — merchant terminal**
> “A merchant verifies it offline with only my public key.”

**0:54 — click Verify & charge**
> “Verified. Paid. The receipt cites the mandate.”

**1:02 — attacker invoice**
> “Now the same invoice names a stranger. Refused: R-one, payee is not a contact.”

**1:12 — frontier card, 3 seconds**
> “We attacked a frontier model. Undefended, it paid half the invoices. Behind this gate: zero. Eighteen of eighteen receipts verify.”

**1:22 — close**
> “Everyone is building agents that can act. This is the one you can hand your wallet to.”

## If a live beat fails

Say “I’ll show you the recorded run” and play the matching 10 seconds from
`docs/demo-owner.mp4`. Do not debug live. Then go straight to the verifier.

## Do not

- do not read the 25 → 37.5 → 50 → 0 table aloud;
- do not claim anti-spoofing — a cloned voice passes;
- do not call the payment real — it is a mock world;
- do not end on the roadmap. End on the wallet line.

## The two beats that win

1. **Stranger voice → R0 refused** (the channel, not the model).
2. **Merchant VERIFIED · PAID** (delegation a third party can check).

Everything else is supporting evidence.
